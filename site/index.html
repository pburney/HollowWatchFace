Hollow

A graveyard-at-night Wear OS watch face: the time sits carved into four stone tombstones, ghosts rise out of the stones on a staggered cycle, a moon-phase icon reads the real lunar phase, and the night sky's color is yours to pick. This is a live preview reading the actual time — no watch required.

Open source (Apache-2.0) — view the source on GitHub. Not a numeral-base face like Radix — a standalone graveyard theme of its own. Weather and the middle complication slots are real Wear OS complications on-device (shown here as placeholders, since a browser has no complication provider to ask). Built with Claude Code.
